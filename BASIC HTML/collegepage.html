ABES ENGINEERING COLLEGE

ABES Engineering College (ABESEC), Ghaziabad, is one of the leading engineering colleges in Uttar Pradesh.
The college offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs and is known for its focus on academics, placements, innovation, and industry-oriented learning.

Department of Computer Science

The CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) department was established in 2020.
The program combines core Computer Science subjects with advanced Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning technologies.
The department offers a 4-year B.Tech degree program and emphasizes practical learning, research, and innovation
